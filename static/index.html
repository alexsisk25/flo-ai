AI writing — local & free

Fixes grammar and punctuation, removes filler words (um, uh, you know), and resolves spoken self-corrections — all on your GPU via a small local model, no cloud. First run downloads it (~1.7 GB), once. Until it's ready, dictation types the raw transcript.
When you fix what Flo typed, it quietly learns — corrected spellings join your dictionary, and recurring style changes shape future cleanups. This is how Flo gets better the more you use it.
